## 🆕 What's new in N63.1.9.6

### ✅ New

| | |
|---|---|
| 🔊 **Sound effects** | 62 sounds for gameplay, combos, warnings, menus and game flow. Everything is embedded in the single HTML file |
| 🎚️ **SFX volume** | New slider in **Options** (set it to 0 to mute) made by 32bits!!!!!|
| 🤖 **Menu demo bot** | A bot plays on the board while you are in the menu. You can turn it off in **Options** |
| 👥 **Credits and testers** | **About** now has Credits, TETR.IO names and a Testers list |

### 🔉 Where the sounds play

| Category | Sounds |
|---|---|
| 🧱 **Gameplay** | Hard drop, Hold, Spin, Clear spin, Line clear (louder for more lines), Quad, Petris, Perfect Clear, Garbage received |
| 🔥 **Combos** | Combo 1 to 16, and Power Combo 1 to 16 when the combo comes from a spin, a quad or a Perfect Clear. A combo break has its own sound |
| ⚠️ **Warnings** | Top-out warning (loops while the icon shows) and Garbage queue warning 1 to 4 |
| 🖱️ **Menu and settings** | Select, Toggle on, Toggle off, Slider, Key rebind, Reset |
| 🎮 **Game flow** | 3, 2, 1 countdown, Start, Pause, Game over, Win, Cheat, Nukie transform |

> [!NOTE]
> Browsers only allow audio after your first click or key press. The audio is OGG, so it works on Chrome, Firefox and Edge. Safari may not play it.

### 🐛 Bug fixes

- None this time

### 🙈 Hidden for now

**Zone**, **Local PvP** and **Spins** are hidden from the menus until they are ready
