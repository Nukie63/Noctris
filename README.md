## 🆕 What's new in N63.1.10.12

### ✅ New

| | |
|---|---|
| 📱 **Mobile and tablet support** | Touch controls with new hand-drawn buttons. Hold left, right or soft drop to keep moving. Pick **PC**, **Tablet** or **Mobile** in **Options** |
| 🎛️ **Button modes** | **Classic** (C1, C2), **Keyboard base** (C1A, C2A) or **Custom**. In Custom, press **Adjust layout** in a game, drag the buttons anywhere (even on top of the board), then press **Done** |
| 📐 **Touch settings** | **Button size** and **Touch DAS** sliders in **Options** |
| 🧭 **Mobile and tablet layout** | Mobile has a portrait menu (**Solo** and **More**) and a game screen with Hold and Next on the sides. Tablet uses the PC layout with the controls in the bottom corners |
| 🌀 **Zone reworked** | Works like Tetris Effect. While Zone is on, every line you make is stored at the bottom and pushes your stack up. When Zone ends (time runs out or you top out), all stored lines clear at once as one big attack |
| ⚡ **Early Zone** | Activate at 50% meter, with less time the lower the meter is. The meter is now on the left of the board. Zone vs Bot has a slider and a Tournament pop-up |
| 🔁 **Restart button** | Next to Options and Menu, and in the Pause screen. Restart, Undo, Options and Menu are now on the left of the board on every device |
| 🌙 **Dark mode** | New toggle in **Options** |
| ⛶ **Full Screen** | New button at the top of **Options** |
| 🧩 **PCs guided mode** | Perfect clear training with a ghost guide, like Finesse |
| 🕹️ **Classic feels like NES** | NES randomizer, NES colors and fixed NES handling |
| 🤖 **Menu demo bot effects** | The demo bot now has the same effects as a real game |

> [!NOTE]
> **Full Screen** works on Android Chrome, tablets and PC browsers. On iPhone Safari it is not available, so use **Add to Home Screen** from the Share menu instead.

### 🐛 Bug fixes

- Undo now keeps the right piece rotation
- The top-out warning sound no longer plays in 4-Wide
- Cheese now leaves one scattered gap per row
- Classic uses fixed NES soft drop and ignores your handling
- Holding left or right on the touch buttons starts moving faster (**Touch DAS**)

### 🙈 Hidden for now

**Local PvP** and **Spins** are hidden from the menus until they are ready
